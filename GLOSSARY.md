# Notebooks

Writing of one's own beside the books one reads: notebooks of pages, written by hand, typed or
spoken, and linked to the knowledge graph.

## Language

**Notebook**:
A named collection of pages, kept in the library as an item of its own, private unless shared.
_Avoid_: Journal, document, file

**Page**:
One sheet of a notebook, with one paper, holding content.
_Avoid_: Sheet, note (for a page inside a notebook)

**Loose note**:
A single page that belongs to no notebook yet and can be filed into one later.
_Avoid_: Memo, scrap, quick note

**Paper**:
What a page is printed with before anything is written on it: lines, squares, dots, staves or nothing.
_Avoid_: Template, background, ruling

**Ruling**:
How far apart a lined paper's lines are: narrow (6.35 mm), college (7.1 mm) or wide (8.7 mm).
_Avoid_: Spacing, line height

**Snapshot**:
The notebook file: what the change log held of a notebook when it was written, readable as a book.
_Avoid_: Save, export (for the notebook file)

**Content**:
What is on a page: ink, typed text, pictures and audio clips.
_Avoid_: Elements, objects, items

**Ink**:
Handwriting and drawing on a page, made of strokes.
_Avoid_: Drawing, sketch, scribble (for the content as a whole)

**Stroke**:
One line of ink from pen down to pen up, with its pressure and the moment it was drawn.
_Avoid_: Path, line, mark

**Tool**:
What ink is written or taken out with: the pen, the highlighter (a broad pen whose colour shows through)
or the eraser (of what it covers, or of whole strokes); and the lasso, which holds strokes.

**Lasso**:
The tool that goes around strokes to hold them, to be moved, recoloured, copied, cut or taken away; what it
does is kept as erasing is: the strokes taken out, new ones put in.
_Avoid_: Marquee, selection (for what the lasso holds)
_Avoid_: Brush, mode

**Text block**:
Typed text placed on a page, set on the paper's lines: plain text or Markdown, never HTML.
_Avoid_: Text box, paragraph, typed note

**Picture**:
An image put on a page from a file, kept with the page so it goes where the page goes; ink may be
written over it.
_Avoid_: Image, attachment, photo (for a picture on a page)

**Above the ink, below the ink**:
Where a text block or a picture lies: over the strokes, or under them, to be written over.
_Avoid_: Layer, z-order (in words a person reads)

**Recording**:
Audio recorded while a page was being written, tied to the strokes drawn during it: each keeps its moment in
the recording, so the ink plays what was said as it was written.
_Avoid_: Audio note, voice memo, clip (for a whole recording)

**Transcript**:
The words a recording was found to hold, each with its moment in the recording where the way that
transcribed it gives them (voxtype doesn't: the transcript isn't *timed*, and a word plays from its
sentence's start).
_Avoid_: Subtitles, captions

**Recognised text**:
The words found in a page's ink, kept with the strokes they were read from, and corrected by hand where
the reading was wrong.
_Avoid_: OCR output, handwriting text

**Selection**:
Strokes of a page chosen with the Select tool, to be read or made a text block on their own.
_Avoid_: Marquee (in words a person reads)

**Place reference**:
A link from a page, or a part of it (strokes, a text block), to a passage of a book, kept by its words as
well as its place, so it is found again when the book's file changes; shown on the page as a chip that
opens the book there.
_Avoid_: Citation, anchor, bookmark

**Lost reference**:
A place reference whose words are no longer in the book's file: kept with its words, sought again in the
next file, never guessed at from its place alone.
_Avoid_: Broken link, orphan

**Notebook scope**:
The part of the knowledge graph that holds what was found in one notebook, linked to things elsewhere: the
things tied to the notebook's book, in the graph of the collections the notebook is in.
_Avoid_: Notes graph, layer

**Export**:
A notebook, or pages of it, saved as a PDF (as the pages look) or as Markdown (their words, pictures beside
them), for reading and keeping outside Omabook. A copy: never read back into the notebook.
_Avoid_: Snapshot (the notebook's own file), backup

**Mention**:
A thing of the knowledge graph the person said a page is about, by hand or by saying yes to one the page
names; it ties the thing to the notebook's book.
_Avoid_: Tag, link, reference (for a mention)
